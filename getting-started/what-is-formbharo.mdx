title: "What FormBharo is"
description: "Voice agents that fill in a form by talking to someone."
---

FormBharo runs voice agents that fill in a form by talking to someone. You write the
questions, the agent asks them out loud, listens to the answers, and saves them.

The API does two jobs. One is building your own page that starts those calls and shows
the answers as they arrive. The other is pulling finished calls into your own systems,
such as a database, a spreadsheet, or a case management tool.

## The two things you work with

An **agent** is a form plus the way it is asked: the questions, their order, and what
the agent says at the start and the end. A **call** is one conversation with one person,
and it holds the answers that conversation produced.
