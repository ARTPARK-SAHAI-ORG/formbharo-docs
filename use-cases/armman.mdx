title: "ARMMAN"
description: "How ARMMAN enrolls pregnant mothers in its health programmes over a phone call."
---

ARMMAN runs maternal and child health programmes across India. Pregnant mothers call FormBharo to fill in the form that enrolls them in those programmes, without waiting for a health worker or visiting the health centre.

Our paper describes this pilot and how the agent was evaluated.
